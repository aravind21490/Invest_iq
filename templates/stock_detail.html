🕯️ Why Traders Use Candlesticks (Candlestick Anatomy)

Educational Lesson

A simple line chart only connects 3:30 PM closing prices. A candlestick reveals the entire intra-day battle between buyers and sellers through 4 critical coordinates:

1. The Real Body (Open vs Close)

The solid box. Green means price rose from Open to Close. Red means price fell from Open to Close.

2. Upper Wick (Shadow)

The thin line extending to the peak. A long upper wick proves buyers tried rallying, but sellers pushed them back.

3. Lower Wick (Shadow)

The thin line extending to the bottom. A long lower wick proves buyers stepped in aggressively to buy the dip.

4. Volume Confirmation

The height of the volume bar underneath confirms whether big institutional funds were active in the move.
